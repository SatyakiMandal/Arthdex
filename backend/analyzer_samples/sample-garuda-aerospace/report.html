Executive Summary & Narrative

SUMMARY

UnlistedZone data is indicative; not a price feed, quote, or offer to deal.

This report examines Garuda Aerospace's indicative price on UnlistedZone between 01 January 2026 and 18 September 2026, alongside what the Indian financial press published about the company over that period. It is a structured timeline, not a statistically validated event study: UnlistedZone's own indicative prices are periodically revised estimates, not daily market-quoted closes, so a day without a revision is not evidence the price was actually unchanged that day — only that no new estimate was published.

Across 261 calendar days in the window, the indicative price was revised 23 times, giving 22 gaps between revisions to examine — roughly one every 11 days on average. Unlike this tool's listed-company reports, no z-score, market-model beta, or permutation-test p-value is computed here: those all assume a daily-moving price to measure unusualness against, which this data does not provide. What follows instead is each revision-to-revision gap, ranked by the size of the change, alongside whatever coverage this run found published in that same window.

A large move here is not necessarily a market reaction: UnlistedZone's indicative series does not appear to be adjusted for corporate actions such as bonus issues, stock splits, or rights issues, any of which mechanically changes the per-share price without changing what the company is worth. A move that looks unusually large is worth checking against the company's own corporate-action history before reading it as sentiment.
