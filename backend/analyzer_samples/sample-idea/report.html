Executive Summary & Narrative

SUMMARY

This report examines Vodafone Idea Limited (IDEA.NS) between 01 January 2026 and 23 August 2026, covering 157 trading days. It is a structured case study, not investment advice, collecting what the Indian financial press published about the company over that period and measuring how the stock moved relative to ^NSEI, to identify days where notable coverage coincided with an unusual company-specific price move. Coincidence in time is not evidence that an article caused a price move.

Price moves are reported as abnormal returns: the company's actual return minus what would have been expected given the benchmark's move that day, using a market model fitted on price history from before this window. This separates the company's own move from the market's, so a day when everything fell is not mistaken for company-specific news.

Across 698 collected items of coverage, 5 candidate days met both tests. The most prominent was 11 May 2026, with an abnormal return of +11.11% alongside broadly neutral coverage.

The top candidate's abnormal return sat 3.5 standard deviations from this stock's own typical daily variation. A permutation test — which does not depend on that z-score's normality assumption — checked this directly: across 20 random comparable-length windows drawn from this stock's own price history, 0.0% were at least as extreme (p = 0.000). It also held up under a range of nearby threshold choices, not just the exact ones configured: flagged in 9 of 9 combinations tried.

The other 152 trading days in the window did not clear both bars at once: 48 days had busy or strongly-toned coverage without a matching unusual price move; 2 days moved unusually without correspondingly unusual coverage; 102 days were routine on both counts. Only a day that clears both — unusual coverage and an unusual price move — becomes a candidate; either alone is not enough on its own.

Of the 5 flagged days, 5 showed a price move in the direction the coverage's tone would suggest and 0 did not. Days where the two disagree are kept in the report rather than filtered out, because they are often the more interesting cases.
